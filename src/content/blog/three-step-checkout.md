How we cut a checkout from eleven steps to three.

## The short version

[PLACEHOLDER: final article copy] We write these notes for founders and product teams who want to understand how we work.

- Start from the outcome you want to measure.
- Remove one step before adding one feature.
- Ship small, review weekly.

> Good work is mostly the removal of things that get in the way.
